I develop tools and methodologies to study processes shaping our built and natural environments. I am currently a Senior Data Scientist at AECOM Advisory and a member of the Urban Analytics Center of Excellence, where my work focuses on applications of spatial data science and machine learning for urban development and infrastructure planning.

This site chronicles my [work](#projects) and [writing](#blog) over the past decade, which I see as deliberate transformations of pixels into priorities, policies and plans.

Previously, I was a graduate researcher at [The University of Chicago](https://voices.uchicago.edu/qrmeth/mapss-qmsa/qmsa-alumni/qmsa-thesis-projects-2023-cohort/) studying topics in quantitative methods, applied math and spatial econometrics. Before that I spent 3.5 years at the [Indian School of Business (ISB)](https://www.isb.edu/), first as a researcher in economics and then as a Senior Product Engineer in the institute research centers for public policy [(BIPP)](https://www.isb.edu/faculty-and-research/bharti-institute-of-public-policy) and digital identity [(DIRI)](https://diri.isb.edu/en.html). I've also held research positions at The Wharton School and Cornell University, where I was a graduate student in the College of Art, Architecture and Planning. Prior to that, I graduated from the School of Planning and Architecture Delhi where I studied urban planning, public policy and design as an undergraduate.

Over the last decade, I've had the opportunity to work in some pretty amazing teams with collaborators across academia, industry and government. My work has been recognized and funded by the [Harvard Climate Change Solutions Fund](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/0a771bbf6873e46cbe11e5e44a801941-0310022024/original/2-Shweta-Bhogale-UCSD-WB-Workshop.pdf), Ernst & Young [Institute for Emerging Markets](https://www.skolkovo.ru/en/centres/iems/), and The [Clarence Stein Institute of Urban and Landscape Studies](https://aap.cornell.edu/academics/crp/stein-institute), and I have been an affiliate of projects supported by the [Bill and Melinda Gates Foundation](https://indiadataportal.com/), [Omidyar Network](https://diri.isb.edu/en/about.html), [The International Land and Forest Tenure Facility](https://thetenurefacility.org/projects/upscaling-community-tenure-rights-in-india-phase-ii/), and [The Knight Foundation](https://knightfoundation.org/articles/detroit-people-first-project/).

When not working, I enjoy long hikes, visiting museums and attending concerts, and occasionally plugging away at math problems.
