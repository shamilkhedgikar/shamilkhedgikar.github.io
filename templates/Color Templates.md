### The "English Heritage" Palette

**Greens (Growth, Urban Planning, Sustainability)**

1.  **Sage Green:** A dried culinary herb color. Very calming and professional.
    *   *Prompt:* "Muted Sage Green" or "Dried Sage"
    *   *Hex:* `#9C9F84`
2.  **Celadon:** A pale, greyish shade of green, like ancient pottery.
    *   *Prompt:* "Pale Celadon" or "Grey-Green"
    *   *Hex:* `#ACE1AF` (but desaturated)
3.  **Eucalyptus:** A cool, slightly blue-ish green. Great for data science.
    *   *Prompt:* "Desaturated Eucalyptus" or "Cool Grey-Green"
    *   *Hex:* `#96B8A3`

**Blues (Data, Policy, Technology)**
4.  **Duck Egg Blue:** A classic English interior color. Soft, greenish-blue.
    *   *Prompt:* "Duck Egg Blue" or "Chalky Blue"
    *   *Hex:* `#AEC6CF`
5.  **Wedgwood Blue:** A traditional matte blue found in pottery.
    *   *Prompt:* "Matte Wedgwood Blue" or "Stoneware Blue"
    *   *Hex:* `#7F93A3`
6.  **Slate Mist:** A grey-blue that feels very industrial and structural.
    *   *Prompt:* "Light Slate Blue" or "Foggy Blue"
    *   *Hex:* `#9DADBC`
7.  **Steel Blue:** A cooler, more metallic (but matte) blue.
    *   *Prompt:* "Pale Steel Blue"
    *   *Hex:* `#B0C4DE`

**Earth & Neutrals (Architecture, History, Foundations)**
8.  **Sandstone:** A warm, yellow-grey. Looks like university stone buildings.
    *   *Prompt:* "English Sandstone" or "Buff"
    *   *Hex:* `#D6C298`
9.  **Taupe:** A dark grey-brown. Very sophisticated and high-end.
    *   *Prompt:* "Muted Taupe" or "Mushroom"
    *   *Hex:* `#B3A698`
10. **Parchment:** An off-white, slightly yellow/grey paper tone.
    *   *Prompt:* "Aged Parchment" or "Creamy Grey"
    *   *Hex:* `#E3DAC9`
11. **Ochre Mist:** A yellow that has been heavily watered down with grey.
    *   *Prompt:* "Pale Ochre" or "Mustard Grey"
    *   *Hex:* `#CCB66D`

**Reds & Purples (Humanities, Sociology, Creative)**
12. **Dusty Rose:** A pink that has been left in the sun. Not candy pink.
    *   *Prompt:* "Dusty Rose" or "Old Pink"
    *   *Hex:* `#DCAE96`
13. **Thistle:** A very pale, purple-grey.
    *   *Prompt:* "Pale Thistle" or "Heather"
    *   *Hex:* `#D8BFD8`
14. **Clay:** A reddish-brown, like brick but faded.
    *   *Prompt:* "Pale Terracotta" or "Faded Brick"
    *   *Hex:* `#C18F80`
15. **Lavender Grey:** Purple with almost all the saturation removed.
    *   *Prompt:* "Grey Lavender"
    *   *Hex:* `#C4C3D0`

### How to prompt these efficiently
When you use the System Instruction I gave you previously, you can swap the **[COLOR THEME]** variable with these phrases for the best results:

*   *"A gradient based on **Muted Sage Green** scaling to white..."*
*   *"A gradient based on **Chalky Duck Egg Blue** scaling to white..."*
*   *"A gradient based on **Desaturated Dusty Rose** scaling to white..."*

**Pro Tip:** If the AI generates colors that are too bright, add the words **"Chalky"** or **"Desaturated"** to the color name. This triggers the matte/pastel look you are aiming for.
